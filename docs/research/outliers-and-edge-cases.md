# Secret Wards edge cases

Likelihood refers to the challenge. **Present** means documented or observed in the bundled data or code. **Expected** means a plausible consequence of documented input variation. **Unconfirmed** means a boundary case with unknown frequency. Severity and difficulty are preliminary judgments. Low difficulty generally means a local rule or check. Medium needs cross-format tests or coordination. High needs a policy decision, model work, or organizer clarification. [Dataset guide](../DATASET.md) · [Observed baseline](codebase-overview.md)

| Case and evidence | Likelihood | Severity | Impact | Mitigation candidate and difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Same words in roles that must be treated differently, such as clinician vs. relative, complaint vs. care-of, prescription schedule vs. ID. [Meaning traps](../DATASET.md#the-traps-what-each-one-tests) | Present | High | Patient leakage or corrupted clinical text | Paired contrast fixtures, scoped role evidence, and exact protected-span checks. Medium |
| New names, missing spaces or labels, mixed scripts and digits, display variants, invisible Unicode. [Unseen round](../DATASET.md#the-unseen-format-round) · [Scoring normalization](../SCORING.md#how-leaks-are-counted-shared-by-several-categories) | Present or expected | High | The detector misses identifiers or edits incorrect spans | Normalize a separate detection view, test offset mapping, and measure by script and channel. Medium |
| Numeric ID inside JSON or a number matching an ID pattern in a clinical observation. [Dataset guide](../DATASET.md#four-hospital-systems-four-ways-of-doing-things) · [JSON cleaner](../../starter/main.py#L123) | Present | High | Direct leakage, type violation or damaging replacement | Classify by path, units, neighboring fields and value type. Check legal replacements and retain known clinical numerics. Medium |
| PII in path components, headers, keys, pointers, metadata or encoded payloads. [Hidden leakage](../DATASET.md#the-traps-what-each-one-tests) · [Locators](../SPEC.md#6-recordsjsonl) | Present for most channels. PII-bearing input pointers and directory components unconfirmed | Critical | Identifiers persist in the release or audit artifact. Renaming can break a locator | Track safe names and original positions internally. Detect collisions and keep distinct output keys. Clarify permissible `loc` fallback. High |
| Homonyms, shared family contacts, local IDs reused by another hospital, or inconsistent bridge records. [Linkability](../DATASET.md#when-must-two-records-be-linked) | Homonyms present. Other cases expected | Critical | A single false join combines two patients' histories and can affect their dates and rarity measures | Scope local IDs by issuer, retain contradictions, and test group compatibility across candidate joins. Medium |
| A fragment contains just a first name and relative, or dates but little identity evidence. [Fragmented identity](../DATASET.md#the-traps-what-each-one-tests) | Present | High | Link recall falls or a weak cluster is misleading | Track why a link is considered supportable. Verify that abstention is permitted and that within-record duration stays valid. Medium |
| Rare diagnosis or long stay becomes unique only once records are pooled. [Privacy scoring](../SCORING.md#3-privacy-attack-arena-25) | Present as a scored scenario | Critical | Patient remains identifiable after direct values are cleaned | Evaluate rarity on patients as well as records and compare permissible reductions in location or date precision. Preserve protected values. High |
| Ambiguous day/month order, leap day, timezone offset, partial date, or dates that span a supported bound. [Input conventions](../DATASET.md#four-hospital-systems-four-ways-of-doing-things) · [FHIR validity scoring](../SCORING.md#5-data-integrity--secure-export-10) | Date conventions present. Other cases expected | High | A stay length or clinical sequence changes, or a date becomes invalid | Track precision, format and date provenance. Use an explicit ambiguity policy and compare original intervals. Medium |
| Format is mislabeled, encoding detection is ambiguous, CSV rows are ragged, or JSON is truncated. [Robustness traps](../DATASET.md#the-traps-what-each-one-tests) | Present | High | Partial or raw output gets published, or a whole file is lost | Check structure and encoding before publication, preserve valid ragged shapes where possible, and classify unsafe data with controlled reasons. Medium |
| Very deep JSON, large file, long CSV field, or massive linkage block with a common name. [Starter file loop](../../starter/main.py#L211) · [Sandbox limits](../SANDBOX.md#equal-resources) | Deep data present. Larger failure thresholds unconfirmed | High | Memory exhaustion, timeout, or catastrophic loss of coverage | Bound per-format resources and candidate pairs. Compare streaming, segmented processing, and explicit quarantine on synthetic stress cases. High |
| FHIR Bundle contains unresolved references, narrative, an attachment, or several patients. [Spec](../SPEC.md#4-output-layout) · [Practice count](codebase-overview.md#baseline-measured-in-this-checkout) | Narratives and attachments documented. Multi-patient inputs unconfirmed | High | Schema-valid JSON may still break clinical references or mix patient identities | Build a consistency check for reference targets and test with an offline R4 validator. Clarify the supported locator granularity. High |
| Two files share an input hash, or sanitization creates two identical output names or JSON keys. [Checker path map](../../check_dev.py#L94) · [JSON key transformation](../../starter/main.py#L123) | Duplicate hashes present. Other collisions expected | High | Input files are undercounted, values overwritten, or a locator points to the wrong output | Track a physical-file ledger internally and allocate safe names before writing. Test repeatability and duplicate counts. Medium |
| Invalid configuration, missing release secret, truncated pseudonym collision, or accidental unchanged date shift between releases. [Starter helpers](../../starter/main.py#L48) · [Release behavior](../SPEC.md#7-behaviour-that-is-scored) | Present as starter conditions. Collisions at full scale unconfirmed | High | Weak privacy, accidental false merge or poor release separation | Validate secret and policy before using them. Use collision detection and test independence over multiple synthetic releases. Medium |
| I/O failure, interrupted run, rerun to a dirty output, or raw temporary artifacts. [Write path](../../starter/main.py#L223) · [Side channels](../SPEC.md#7-behaviour-that-is-scored) | Expected failure class, incidence unknown | Critical | Partial release, unsafe diagnostic text, or stale data escapes | Stage only sanitized output and publish completed artifacts together where practical. Check controlled errors and verify no raw artifacts or secrets persist. Medium |
| Path names clash after normalization, or nonregular input entries appear in a future export. [Input traversal](../../starter/main.py#L223) · [Release path rules](../SPEC.md#4-output-layout) | Unconfirmed | High | A write can leave the intended destination or a read can go beyond the intended input tree | Verify regular inputs and destination containment, and test normalization collisions on synthetic paths. Medium |
| Healthy files or rare cohorts are disproportionately suppressed. [Record retention](../SCORING.md#4-clinical-utility-20) | Expected policy risk | High | Aggregate and cohort analyses become misleading | Measure retention by cohort and by format with an input-based denominator and document affected groups. Medium |
| Source includes valid data that resembles control characters in a JSON pointer or introduces Unicode-equivalent labels. [JSON Pointer encoding](../../starter/main.py#L178) | Expected | Medium | Record mapping drift or false collisions | Test reserved pointer characters and preserve location identity independently of display text. Low |

The highest-priority tests for unfamiliar inputs are unfamiliar role labels and names, file-level channels, false merges from conflicting clues, and clinical preservation after date or format changes. The local self-check's success signals should be complemented by small assertions around each of those cases. [Feasibility and experiments](feasibility-analysis.md)
